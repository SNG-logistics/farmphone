## 2. Unit Economics & Gross Margin Target

- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) Per Rendered Video (25.0s MP4)**:

  - Storage (MinIO / S3 Bandwidth & Assets): ~0.20 ฿ / video
  - Creative QA Processing: ~0.05 ฿ / video
  - Total Estimated Cost per Video: **~0.40 ฿ / video**
- **Average Revenue Per Video (Business Tier)**: 3,990 ฿ / 100 = **39.90 ฿ / video**
- **Target Gross Margin**: **> 85%**
